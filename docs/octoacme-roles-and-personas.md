# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA Leads

### Role Summary
QA Leads define and govern the quality strategy for a project, ensuring releases meet acceptance criteria and customer expectations before production deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage goals, and release quality gates
- Coordinate test planning across unit, integration, and end-to-end validation
- Triage defects and work with engineering to prioritize fixes
- Verify that requirements and acceptance criteria are testable and measurable
- Partner with Product and Project Managers to assess readiness for milestones and releases

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve confidence in delivery quality and release readiness
- Align validation effort with customer and business risk

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on defect triage, regression coverage, and testability feedback
- Collaborates with Product Managers on acceptance criteria and validation criteria
- Reports quality status and risks to Project Managers for milestone and release decisions

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and release readiness reviews
- Defect triage discussions and validation sign-offs
- Status reporting integrated into sprint and release check-ins

---

## Security & Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security & Compliance Partners help ensure the project follows organizational policies, risk requirements, and secure delivery practices throughout the lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Review project risks related to security, privacy, and compliance
- Validate that controls, approvals, and guardrails are in place before release
- Partner with engineering to assess vulnerabilities and remediation plans
- Support stakeholder communication for high-risk findings or policy issues
- Help define secure release criteria and escalation paths for incidents

### Goals
- Protect customer trust and organizational risk posture
- Reduce preventable security and compliance issues
- Make security work visible and actionable earlier in delivery

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure design, testing, and remediation priorities
- Works with Product Managers to balance speed, risk, and customer expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on escalation, risk tracking, and release gating

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and compliance checkpoints
- Risk and mitigation updates in project status discussions
- Incident escalation and follow-up communications when needed

---

## Release Managers / DevOps Engineers

### Role Summary
Release Managers and DevOps Engineers coordinate deployment readiness, operational stability, and recovery plans so releases can be executed safely and consistently.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment windows, rollback readiness, and release communications
- Maintain CI/CD and environment readiness for testing and production release
- Validate service health, dependencies, and monitoring before and after deployment
- Support incident response and recovery when release issues arise
- Partner with engineering and PMs to sequence releases with stakeholder expectations

### Goals
- Reduce release friction and operational risk
- Improve deployment predictability and observability
- Shorten time to recover when production issues occur

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate deployment readiness and environment health
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release timing, communication, and risk mitigation
- Aligns with Product Managers on customer impact and launch readiness milestones

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and readiness reviews
- Deployment status updates and rollback decision points
- Post-deployment verification and incident follow-up

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaisons

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect product delivery to customer experience, ensuring feedback, service readiness, and operational issues are visible to the delivery team.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, bug trends, and support escalations
- Help prioritize issues that affect user trust, adoption, or retention
- Coordinate readiness for launch communications, training, and support coverage
- Share customer-impact stories with Product and Engineering leaders
- Support post-release follow-up and issue tracking after go-live

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption outcomes
- Connect customer reality to delivery decisions and prioritization
- Reduce avoidable support escalations after launch

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with customer insights and support trends for prioritization
- Helps Project Managers track stakeholder impacts and communication needs
- Partners with Developers on reproducing bugs and validating customer-facing fixes

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and service health updates
- Support escalation summaries and launch readiness check-ins
- Post-release feedback loops and improvement planning

---

## Data / Analytics Owners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Owners define how product outcomes and operational performance are measured, enabling evidence-based decision-making and continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation, and reporting requirements
- Ensure the right data is collected and trusted for product decisions
- Monitor adoption, quality, performance, and business impact metrics
- Partner with Product and PM teams to interpret outcomes and make trade-offs
- Support experiment design and post-launch learning reviews

### Goals
- Move from opinion-based decisions to evidence-based delivery
- Improve visibility into product health and business impact
- Support iterative learning and measurement of value

### Typical Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides Product Managers with objective evidence for roadmap decisions
- Works with Developers to define instrumentation and reporting requirements
- Supports Project Managers in milestone tracking and stakeholder reporting

### Typical Communication
- KPI reviews, dashboard updates, and quarterly or milestone reporting
- Experiment and analysis summaries for product and delivery discussions
- Insights used in retrospectives and improvement planning

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles make project ownership, execution, risk handling, and communication more explicit across the full delivery lifecycle.
- This expanded model helps teams clarify not only who is responsible for work, but also who is accountable for quality, security, release readiness, customer impact, and measurement.
